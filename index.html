PROJECT / 01

114/900 Newtonian Optical Telescope Assembly

MY ROLE Optical Layout & Mechanical CAD
TIMELINE Oct 2025 – Jan 2026

End-to-end mechanical design of a 114 mm aperture, 900 mm focal length Newtonian reflector. Optimized tube baffling, 4-vane secondary spider stiffness, and a zero-backlash rack-and-pinion focuser within a 24.5% central obstruction budget.

  • SolidWorks
  • Optical Raytracing
  • DFM / 3D Printing
  • Tolerance Stack
Quick engineering notes
  • Sized secondary diagonal minor axis at 28 mm (24.5% linear obstruction) to preserve planetary contrast while illuminating a 14 mm full-field focal circle.
  • Modeled 1.5 mm rolled steel vs. carbon-reinforced PLA tube deflection under a 1.8 kg eyepiece/camera load; limited tip sag below 0.12 mm.
  • Designed 3-point kinematic spring-loaded primary mirror cell with 120° nylon radial edge pads to prevent astigmatic pinching.

PROJECT / 02

ZVS High-Voltage Flyback Plasma Driver

MY ROLE Power Electronics & Embedded Control
TIMELINE Feb 2026 – Apr 2026

Self-oscillating Mazzilli Zero-Voltage Switching (ZVS) driver powering a salvaged BSC25-F1118N CRT flyback transformer at ~48 kHz. Paired with an ESP32-C3 resistive voltage-divider telemetry loop for bus under-voltage lockout and thermal fault shutdown.

  • Power Electronics
  • ZVS Topology
  • ESP32-C3
  • High-Voltage Safety
Quick engineering notes
  • Tuned 0.68 µF polypropylene resonant tank capacitor against a 5+5 turn center-tapped primary to keep switching transitions strictly at zero drain-source voltage.
  • Added 12 V Zener gate clamps and UF4007 fast-recovery cross-coupling diodes to protect IRFP250N gates from transient ringing during arc ignition.
  • Scaled 12 V DC bus telemetry via a 47 kΩ / 9.4 kΩ divider into the ESP32-C3 12-bit ADC with software RC filtering to trigger soft shutdown above 65 °C heatsink temperature.

PROJECT / 03

Inverted Double Pendulum on a Cart (LQR)

MY ROLE Dynamic Modeling & Control Synthesis
TIMELINE May 2026 – Present

Nonlinear Euler-Lagrange formulation and Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR) stabilization for an underactuated 6-DOF inverted double pendulum on a motorized cart. Validated closed-loop disturbance rejection and actuator saturation bounds via RK4 simulation in Python.

  • Lagrangian Dynamics
  • State-Space LQR
  • Python / SciPy
  • RK4 Simulation
Quick engineering notes
  • Derived coupled nonlinear inertia M(q), Coriolis C(q, q̇), and gravity G(q) matrices symbolically and linearized around the unstable upright equilibrium θ₁ = θ₂ = 0.
  • Verified full state controllability (rank(C) = 6) before solving the Continuous Algebraic Riccati Equation (CARE) with Bryson's rule weighting.
  • Evaluated impulse recovery up to ±8° initial link perturbations while capping horizontal cart drive force within a ±25 N actuator envelope.
