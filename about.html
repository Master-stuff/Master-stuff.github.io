ENGINEERING PROFILE / BACKGROUND

Building physical intuition through CAD, hardware, and math.

I am Abdallah Bouaicha, a Mechanical Engineering student at ENSAM Rabat (Class of 2029) based in Rabat-Témara, Morocco. My work sits at the intersection of classical mechanical design, mechatronics, and analytical control systems.

Rather than treating software simulation and physical fabrication as separate worlds, I treat every project as a single closed loop: deriving the governing physics first, translating constraints into manufacturable CAD assemblies or power topologies, and testing real prototypes until empirical measurements match the model.

Design Philosophy

Good engineering leaves very little hidden. Whether sizing a Newtonian telescope's secondary mirror against diffraction contrast limits, tuning a zero-voltage switching resonant tank, or balancing a 6-DOF inverted double pendulum within actuator saturation bounds, every millimeter, microfarad, and gain coefficient should trace back to a clear physical trade-off.
